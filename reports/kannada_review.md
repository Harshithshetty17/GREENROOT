# GREENROOT — Kannada review sheet

Please read each Kannada line against its English and mark anything that is wrong, unclear, or that a farmer would not say. **Where you are unsure, say so** — leaving a line flagged is more useful than guessing.

This is agronomic advice people act on in a field, so a wrong word has a real cost. The English is the source of truth; the Kannada was written by someone who does not speak the language.
